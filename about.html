About us

Our story

Karabo's Confectionery began as a home kitchen hobby, baking cakes for family birthdays and friends' celebrations. As word spread, orders grew from a handful of neighbours into a steady stream of requests for weddings, corporate events, and milestone birthdays across the community.

Today, every cake, cupcake, and cookie is still made by hand, using the same care and attention that started it all.

Our mission

To bake fresh, high-quality confectionery using the best ingredients, with love and attention to detail in every order.

Our vision

My vision is to become Polokwane’s most trusted bakery for custom cakes, known for creativity and heart, as we grow into a brand that makes celebrations sweeter across South Africa.

Meet the baker

Karabo founded the business out of a love for baking and design, combining flavour with creative, custom presentation for every order.
